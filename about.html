About THRAZHA International Inc.

At THRAZHA, Bridging Fields Worldwide isn't just our mission—it's our commitment to creating sustainable connections across industries.

Connecting producers and distributors

THRAZHA International Inc. is a premier Canadian agricultural trading company specializing in the global trade of premium palm oil, sunflower seeds, cocoa, sesame, and other agricultural commodities. Based in Ontario, we connect quality producers with trusted distributors worldwide, ensuring seamless supply chain operations and reliable product delivery.
